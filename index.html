Controls
Keyboard
W / ↑ gas · S / ↓ brake, reverse · A D / ← → steer · Space e-brake · C camera (chase, far chase, inside, bonnet, overhead) · R back on track · P pause

Scoring
Points grow with slip angle and speed. Keep the slide going to raise the multiplier. A wall hit or leaving the asphalt drops the chain.
